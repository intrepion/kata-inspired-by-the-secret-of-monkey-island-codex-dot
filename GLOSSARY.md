# Last Laugh Lagoon

The people and objects of a community adventure about reclaiming public joy.

## Language

**Laughing Bell**: The lagoon's communal bell, rung to begin its annual toast.
_Avoid_: Treasure, loot

**Permit**: The customs clerk's authorization to visit the Paper Fort.
_Avoid_: Ticket, pass

**Signal**: The Weather House's light declaring safe access to the floating fort.
_Avoid_: Beacon repair

**Wind scoop**: A cloth paddle that catches wind to turn the weather vane.
_Avoid_: Sail, fan

**Corked cup**: A floating cup used to retrieve the lens from a shallow pool.
_Avoid_: Boat, raft

**Appeal**: A three-question conversation proving the bell serves the whole lagoon.
_Avoid_: Insult duel, combat
